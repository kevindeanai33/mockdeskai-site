The Problem

Simple mockup changes shouldn't take hours

A sales rep needs to swap a headline on a mockup. They email the designer, wait for a revision, request another round of changes, and wait again. A five-minute edit turns into a two-day bottleneck. Multiply that across a team and hundreds of accounts, and you're losing deals to turnaround time.

The Solution

Edit mockups yourself, in seconds, with AI

MockDeskAI puts design editing power directly in the hands of sales reps. Open a PSD, describe your change in plain language, and the AI handles the rest. No Photoshop skills needed. No waiting on designers. You stay in control of the client conversation.

How It Works

Three steps. No learning curve.

1

Upload your PSD

Drop in the Photoshop mockup file you received from your design team. MockDeskAI reads every layer, text element, and group.

2

Describe your edit

Type what you want changed in plain English. "Change the headline to Summer Sale." "Hide the background layer." "Set the logo opacity to 50%."

3

Export the result

Review the updated design in the live preview, make more changes if needed, then export as PNG or JPG. Send it to your client in minutes, not days.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Text Editing

Change any text layer by describing what you want. Headlines, subtext, pricing, dates -- just say it.

Layer Control

Show, hide, or adjust opacity on any layer or group. The AI understands your PSD structure.

Undo / Redo

Made a mistake? Step backward and forward through your edit history with full undo support.

PNG & JPG Export

Export your finished design in the format you need. Ready to send to the client or drop into a presentation.

Works Offline

MockDeskAI is a desktop app. Your PSD files never leave your machine. No cloud uploads, no latency.

Your Data Stays Local

Design files are processed on your computer. Client artwork and proprietary designs never touch a server.
